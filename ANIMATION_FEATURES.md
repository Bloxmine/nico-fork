# Animation Maker - New Features

## Animation Speed Slider

Control the playback speed of your animation preview with the speed slider.

- **Location**: Next to the Play/Stop buttons
- **Range**: 10% - 200% of original speed
- **Default**: 100% (normal speed)
- **Usage**: Adjust the slider while playing to see your animation at different speeds

### How it works
- Lower percentages = slower playback
- Higher percentages = faster playback
- This only affects preview playback, not the saved frame durations

## Selection Tool & Custom Brushes

Create custom brushes from your artwork by selecting regions and saving them.

### Using the Selection Tool

1. **Activate Selection Mode**: Click the "Select" button in the mode toolbar
2. **Draw a Selection**: Click and drag on the canvas to create a selection box
3. **Save as Brush**: Click the "💾 Save as Brush" button that appears
4. **Name Your Brush**: Enter a name when prompted

### Using Custom Brushes

1. **Switch to Custom Mode**: Click the "Custom" button in the brush shape toolbar
2. **Select a Brush**: Choose from the dropdown that appears
3. **Paint**: The custom brush will paint your saved pattern

### Custom Brush Features

- **Pattern-based**: The exact pixels you selected are saved
- **Center-aligned**: The brush paints centered on your cursor
- **Reusable**: Save as many custom brushes as you need
- **Named**: Each brush is saved with a unique name
- **Dropdown selection**: Easy access to all your custom brushes

### Tips

- Select interesting patterns or characters to reuse
- Custom brushes work with both Paint and Erase modes
- The brush size slider doesn't affect custom brushes (they use their saved size)
- Each custom brush shows its dimensions in the dropdown (e.g., "MyBrush (5x7)")

## Workflow Examples

### Creating a Custom Character Brush

1. Draw a small character (e.g., a smiley face)
2. Click "Select" mode
3. Drag a box around your character
4. Click "Save as Brush"
5. Name it "Smiley"
6. Switch to "Custom" brush mode
7. Select "Smiley" from dropdown
8. Paint multiple copies across your canvas!

### Testing Animation Speed

1. Create a simple animation with a few frames
2. Click "Play"
3. Adjust the speed slider to find the perfect timing
4. Note the percentage that looks best
5. Adjust individual frame durations as needed for final export
